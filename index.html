01 / Identity & Vision

New Media Design & Tactile Craft

I'm a New Media Design student and multidisciplinary artist originating from Istanbul and currently based in Sweden. Bridging vibrant cultural perspectives with Scandinavian editorial minimalism, I craft human-centered digital experiences rooted in tactile aesthetics, intentional whitespace, and sharp narrative pacing.

Roots: Istanbul ✦ Sweden (CET) Focus: Magazine Blog, UX/UI, Editorial & DJ
